# September rules and human authorship

The September schedule supplied in ../1.txt supersedes the July dates bundled with osc2026-guide: submission and acceptance close September 24, 2026. The implementation is AI-assisted original MoonBit code, not a translation of SciPy, lmfit or GPL domain projects. The final proposal must be written and approved by the applicant; this repository will provide a one-page factual outline, not claim that an AI-authored proposal satisfies the human-writing rule. Remote creation, push and Mooncakes publication are deferred until local completion as requested.
